Recommended Final Palette

Powder White + Teal + Sand accent

Teal is the language of intelligence tools — Tableau, Palantir, Mixpanel all use it. It reads as trustworthy, precise, and modern. Powder White keeps it clean and airy. Sand as a subtle warm accent stops it looking cold. This combination will look credible in a VC deck, a university presentation, and a commercial SaaS landing page.

Primary: #1E7A78 Teal Background: #F7F5F0 Powder White Accent: #D4B896 Sand Dark: #0D3D3C Deep Teal Text: #1A1A1A Near-Black

3 Fresh Alternatives — If You Want Something New

Option A — Midnight + Gold

Deep navy background with warm gold accents

Vibe: Premium intelligence platform. Feels like Bloomberg Terminal for SMBs.

Good for: Enterprise clients, high-ticket SaaS, investor decks.

Hex: #1A1F3A · #C9A84C · #F7F5F0
Premium Enterprise

Option B — Slate Blue + Warm Coral

Professional blue base, warm coral for alerts

Vibe: Modern analytics tool — coral pops perfectly for anomaly alerts.

Good for: Dashboard-first products, alert-heavy UX, young enterprise.

Hex: #3D5A73 · #E07B54 · #F7F5F0
Modern Alert-Ready

Option C — Charcoal + Teal + Sand

Dark base keeps brown warmth, teal stays modern

Vibe: Sophisticated dark-mode analytics. Warm enough to feel human, sharp enough to sell.

Good for: Dark-mode dashboard, logo, landing page hero.

Hex: #2C3333 · #1E7A78 · #D4B896 · #F7F5F0
Dark Mode Teal Warm
